# Story tools

## Story twin

The Story twin is the room's model of your story. DraftPilot derives it from your working draft and
keeps it up to date:

- **Characters**, from dialogue cues: how many lines each speaks, in how many scenes, and where
  each first appears.
- **Locations**, from scene headings: interior or exterior, the times of day used, and the scenes
  each appears in.

It refreshes a few seconds after you stop editing. You can also click **Refresh from the draft**.
Write your own note on any character or location, and the twin keeps it: derived facts update around
your note, but your words are never overwritten. Every agent reads a short brief of the twin before
it works, so the room always knows who is in your story.

## Writer twin

The Writer twin holds who you are and how you work. It is made up of:

- your profile, style notes, preferences and things to avoid (**Settings → Writer profile**);
- memories you pin, whether for every project or for just one;
- what the room has learned from your decisions, meaning how often you approve or reject its
  proposals.

The room gives the Writer twin priority over any specialist's opinion.

## Knowledge graph

The knowledge graph (**Creative context → Knowledge graph**) holds the Story twin's characters and
locations, plus any canon, themes and relationships you add. Edits are versioned, and connections
between nodes are yours to draw.

## Story studio

The story studio holds your development artifacts: the brief (logline), outline, characters, canon,
timeline and more. Room workflows propose changes to these artifacts. For example, **Notes →
outline** proposes a logline for the Brief and beats for the Outline. When an artifact another one
depends on changes, the dependent artifact is marked stale.

## Beat board

**Beat board** (in the workspace header) lays the Outline's beats out in three acts:

- move a beat to an earlier or later act with ← and →, and reorder beats within an act with ↑ and ↓;
- rewrite a beat's title or summary, add beats, or remove them;
- click **Save outline** to write a new version of the Outline artifact.

**Outline → scenes** drafts from exactly these beats.

## Creative context

Context workflows generate cited, review-only suggestions: reference scenes, visual language, camera,
lighting, palettes and continuity notes. See [Context workflows](context-workflows.md).
