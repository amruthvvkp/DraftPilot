# Develop a story

Every project moves through four stages, shown as a stepper at the top of the project:
**Storyline → Characters → Outline → Script**. Each stage shows how complete it is. You can do every
stage by hand, ask the chat to help with it, or let the room run the whole pipeline and approve its
work stage by stage. All three paths write the same records, so you can mix them freely.

## Storyline

The **Storyline** page holds the title, logline, premise (the dramatic question), synopsis, theme
and tone, plus free description and outline notes. Save with the button or ++ctrl+s++. If the
project changed elsewhere since you opened it (another tab, or an approved proposal), DraftPilot
keeps your edits and asks whether to save your version or discard it.

## Characters

The **Characters** page lists the cast with each character's role and where they came from: yours,
derived from the script by the Story twin, or added by an approved agent proposal. A character's
page holds their whole profile:

- **who they are:** summary, bio, voice, aliases, tags;
- **what drives them:** want (the conscious goal), need (the inner truth), motive, objective, flaw;
- **how they change:** the arc summary, and a **timeline** of moments in story order, each with when
  it happens, what they want in that moment and how they feel, optionally linked to a scene or beat;
- **relationships** with the rest of the cast.

Characters who speak in the script appear automatically (with their line counts and scenes). Once you
edit one, it is yours: the Story twin never overwrites what you wrote. **Locations** work the same way.

## Outline

The **Outline** page shows your acts side by side, with their beats. Add, rename, reorder or delete
acts (a deleted act's scenes move to the act you choose). Drag beats between acts, or use the arrow
keys on a beat's handle. A beat links to the characters it moves and to the scenes that dramatise it;
**Create scene from beat** writes the scene heading for you and links it.

## Script

The **Script** is the screenplay editor ([Writing in the studio](editor.md)). Create the first scene
from the empty page, move scenes up, down or into another act, and delete them. Change any block's
type directly.

## The context map

**Context map** shows, word for word, the brief every agent reads before it works: the storyline,
the cast with their motives, objectives, arcs and timelines, the key places, the outline beats and
the canon. Next to it, it lists what is still empty at each stage, with a link to fill it. What is
missing here is what the agents will have to guess.

## Let the room do a stage, or all of them

In the chat, ask for what you need ("give Karl a timeline", "outline act two"), or open the
**Writers' room** and start **Develop the story** with your idea. The pipeline runs:

1. the story architect drafts the storyline;
2. the character specialist proposes the cast;
3. one specialist per lead traces their timeline, in parallel;
4. the story editor writes the outline and the script doctor critiques it until it holds;
5. scene writers draft the first beats as scenes, in parallel;
6. the coverage reader reviews the result.

After each stage the pipeline **waits for you**. Its proposals appear in the Writers' room and in the
chat; approve, edit or reject them, and the next stage builds only on what you approved (a character
you reject gets no timeline). Choose which stages to run, how many characters and how many beats to
draft when you start it.
